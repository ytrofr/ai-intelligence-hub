What we should adopt next

Every candidate the Stack Ledger has scored or slotted, in one place: what it would improve, effort/effect/time/impact/risk coloured by whether the number is GOOD news (the header arrow says which way), one rolled-up score, and the four checks that stop an adoption - evidence, licence, cost, hardware fit. Colour is teal/amber/orange, not green/red, and never travels alone: every mark carries a shape and a word. Its sibling, the Stack Ledger, is the full record of everything we depend on or have measured against; this page is only the rows there is something left to decide about.

score = (effect + impact + (6 − effort) + (6 − time) + (6 − risk)) / 25 × 100 — modules/ledger.js:scoreTotal(), the same formula the Stack Ledger uses. Colour bands: score 80+ / 65-79 / below 65; a dimension is favourable at 4-5 after inverting the three cost dimensions (effort, time, risk — lower is better). Licence is read from each artifact's own LICENSE file and cleared against the permissive allowlist in modules/slot-gate.js; anything it does not recognise is flagged, never passed.
